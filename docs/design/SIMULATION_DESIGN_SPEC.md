# CMO Simulator: Gameplay & Design Spec

## 1. Educational Layer & Mechanisms
### 1.1 Digital Twin "Sandbox"
Instead of blindly spending millions, players can unlock "Synthetic Personas" in a planning stage to dry-run limited budgets and view localized CTR reactions. This teaches Agile Marketing and "Fail Fast" methodologies.

### 1.2 "Board of Directors" AI & Stress Meters
Three pressure meters act as gameplay constraints:
- **CEO (Growth Focus)**: Demands increasing market share and bolder category creation.
- **CFO (Profit Focus)**: Demands high MROI, low CAC, and tight financial margins.
- **CMO / Team (Sanity Focus)**: Demands stable workloads and skill alignment to prevent burnout.
*Mechanic: If any meter drops to zero, the game triggers a "Board Meeting" event defending the strategy, or results in a Game Over firing.*

### 1.3 Flow Matrix for Team Management
Tracking leadership decisions plotting team focus on a Challenge vs. Skill 2D matrix (based on Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi’s Flow). 
- High challenge + low skill = Anxiety & Productivity Drop.
- Low challenge + high skill = Boredom & Attrition.

## 2. Executive Decision Levers
### 2.1 Quality vs. Speed Trade-off
Skipping R&D time to rush a product (Time-to-Market leverage) increases $P(Recall)$ exponentially.
- **Lesson**: First-mover advantage vs. Brand Reputation Risk.
- **Effect**: A recall hits the Brand Equity variable, lowering the effectiveness of every ad dollar spent.

### 2.2 Strategy Tech Tree (MarTech Stack)
Upgrading the infrastructure (e.g., AI Personalization) costs fixed budgets overhead ($500k/quarter, taking 2 quarters) but yields a permanent conversion multiplier (e.g., +15%).
- **Lesson**: Capital investment vs. Operating expenses.

### 2.3 Hiring vs. Outsourcing
Hiring an in-house team increases fixed costs (moving the Break-Even Point) but lowers variable cost per campaign compared to an external agency.
- **Lesson**: Operating leverage and Risk Resilience during market recessions.

## 3. The Dashboard UI & Visualizations
- **Sonar Radar (Perceptual Mapping)**: Displaying the player's brand dot moving relative to the pulsing gold target of the customer's "Ideal Point".
- **Risk Indicator Slider**: A simple UI control dictating brand aggression vs. margin stability, feeding directly into the Stochastic probability engine (higher aggression = higher standard deviation of sales).
- **Gantt Campaign Sequencer**: A draggable track revealing "Synergy Bonuses" when sequencing Performance campaigns immediately following Brand Awareness flights.

## 4. The Crisis Engine (Adaptive Difficulty)
The simulator detects if the player's "Competency Score" is too steady, unleashing randomized constraints such as:
- **Supply Chain Shock**: Inventory drops to 0 for a quarter, forcing the CMO to pivot messaging to wait-lists.
- **Customer data incident**: Trust score and targeting efficiency fall sharply until the CMO funds a credible response (comms, customer remedies, and security hardening).
- **Price War**: Aggressive competitors slash prices. Players matching the price cut trigger a new Nash Equilibrium where total market value shrinks.
